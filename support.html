What BubbleDock is

A lightweight floating launcher for website shortcuts. Add a URL, customize the bubble, and open it in your browser. Themes and backgrounds stay on your phone.

What we don’t do

  • No account or login
  • No ads in the current version
  • No cloud sync of your bubbles

FAQ

How do I add a link?

From the home screen, use the Add bubble, paste or type a full URL (including https:// when possible), then set a name and icon. Save to place it on your dock.

Why do I need internet permission?

BubbleDock checks if you are online before opening links, and may fetch a favicon/icon for sites you add. Opening a link uses your browser and needs a network connection.

Why does the app ask for photos / gallery access?

Only if you choose a custom home background from your gallery. The image is stored in the app’s local files. We do not upload your photo library.

A link won’t open / I see “no internet”

Confirm Wi‑Fi or mobile data is on, then retry. If you are online but a specific site fails, try the same URL in Chrome. Some sites block non-browser clients; BubbleDock opens links in your system browser.

How do I delete a bubble or reset the look?

Use edit/delete on custom bubbles from the home screen, and open Settings to change theme or background. Uninstalling the app removes local BubbleDock data on that device.

Is my data shared or sold?

No. See the Privacy Policy for full details on on-device storage and third-party icon/connectivity requests.
