Privacy Policy

BubbleDock is a floating link launcher. It keeps your shortcuts on your device and does not require an account. This page explains what the app accesses and why.

Effective: July 31, 2026 Last updated: July 31, 2026 App: BubbleDock (Android)

1. Who we are

BubbleDock is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”).

Contact for privacy questions: YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com

Replace YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com with the same email you use in Google Play Console (Store listing → Contact details).

2. What BubbleDock does

BubbleDock lets you save website links as floating bubbles, customize icons and names, choose a home background (including a photo from your gallery), and open links in your device’s browser. Preferences and layout are stored locally on your phone.

3. Information we process

BubbleDock does not create user accounts and does not operate its own backend server that stores your data.

3.1 Data stored on your device

  • Link URLs, display names, icon choices, and bubble positions
  • Theme and background preferences
  • Optional custom background image you pick from your photo library (copied into app storage)
  • Temporary draft data while adding a link

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example by taking a screenshot or backing up your phone).

3.2 Information accessed with permissions

  • Internet / network access — to check connectivity, download favicons or icons for links you add, and open websites you choose.
  • Photos / media (gallery) — only when you pick a custom background image. We do not upload your photo library to our servers.

3.3 Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or login credentials (no sign-in)
  • Precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera capture for posting
  • Advertising IDs for ads (BubbleDock does not show ads)
  • Analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the current version

4. Network requests and third parties

When you use BubbleDock, your device may contact third-party services:

  • Favicon / icon providers (for example Google Favicons or DuckDuckGo icon endpoints) — they may receive the domain of a URL you added so an icon can be fetched.
  • Connectivity checks — brief requests to public endpoints (such as Google connectivity checks) to detect if you are online.
  • Websites you open — links launch in your external browser; those sites’ own privacy policies apply.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising or personalized marketing.

5. Why we process data (purposes)

  • App functionality — save and show your launcher bubbles
  • Personalization — theme, layout, and background you choose
  • Reliability — detect offline state before opening links

6. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove it in the app (for example deleting a bubble or changing the background) or uninstall BubbleDock. Uninstalling removes app-local storage for this app on that device.

Because we do not host your launcher data on our servers, there is no cloud profile for us to delete remotely. If you have a privacy question, email YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com.

7. Children’s privacy

BubbleDock is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help clarify how local data works on the device.

8. Security

We rely on your device’s OS storage protections for locally saved preferences and images. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Avoid storing sensitive secrets (passwords, private tokens) inside link names or URLs if possible.

9. International users

BubbleDock is distributed via Google Play. Network requests to third-party icon or connectivity services may be processed in the regions where those providers operate. Local app data remains on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of BubbleDock after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
